This app includes derived, compact JSON representations of the word timing
protobuf files for Noreen Siddiq (Duri an Abu Amr) and Alzain Mohammad Ahmad
(Murattal). Original word indices and timestamps are preserved, including
invalid/zero-duration ranges. Recordings remain hosted by the publisher.

Changes by Manarat Al-Muslim, 2026-09-10: protobuf timing metadata converted
to [zero-based word index, start milliseconds, end milliseconds] tuples.
Playback boundary recovery is separate app logic and does not modify the
publisher's word timestamps. See docs/QURAN_AUDIO.md for evidence and limits.
